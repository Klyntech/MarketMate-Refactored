# Course 2 — Core Smart Money Concepts

## Prerequisites: Course 1 (or equivalent knowledge)

| Module | Title | Practical Exercise |
|--------|-------|---------------------|
| 2.1 | What is Smart Money? | Written reflection: "Why might a bank want to push price below a swing low?" |
| 2.2 | Liquidity Sweeps | Identify a sweep on a real XAUUSD H4 chart; mark the swept level |
| 2.3 | Order Blocks (OB) | Draw an OB on a provided chart; explain why it qualifies |
| 2.4 | Fair Value Gaps (FVG) | Find an FVG on a 15‑minute XAUUSD chart; measure the gap |
| 2.5 | Break of Structure (BOS) | Identify BOS on M15; explain why it supports the HTF bias |
| 2.6 | Change of Character (CHoCH) | Identify CHoCH on M5; explain why it warns of a potential turn |
| 2.7 | OB vs. FVG – Prioritisation | Compare two setups; justify which entry zone is higher probability |
| 2.8 | Supply & Demand Refinement | Mark fresh supply/demand zones on a real chart; explain their validity |
| 2.9 | Multi‑Timeframe SMC Alignment | Full narrative on a multi‑TF chart set: sweep → OB → BOS |

## Assessment
- Quiz: 20 questions, 70% to pass
- Practical submission: Annotate a chart with at least one sweep, one OB/FVG, and one BOS/CHoCH. Groq AI provides personalised feedback.
- Badge upon completion: SMC Practitioner
