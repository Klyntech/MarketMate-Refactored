# Professional Trader Certification

## Course 5 — Final Certification Exam

### Module 5.1 — Final Certification Exam
- 40 questions across all four courses
- Practical chart‑marking challenge
- Trade‑plan submission
- 80% to pass
- Not timed
- Correct answers are never revealed after failure

## Certificate
Upon passing the final exam:
- Student earns the "MarketMate SMC Trader" certificate
- PDF generated via weasyprint from an HTML template
- Contains: full name, "MarketMate SMC Trader" title, completion date, unique certificate ID, MarketMate logo
- Certificate ID publicly verifiable at GET /verify-certificate/{cert_id}
- Educational‑only language: "This certificate confirms educational completion only; it does not authorise the holder to provide financial advice or execute trades on behalf of others."

## Certificate Value
- Immediate: unlocks certified community, priority Q&A, funded‑trader‑programme entry ticket
- Long‑term: public verification, partner outreach to prop firms/brokers after 50+ certified traders, success‑story case studies
