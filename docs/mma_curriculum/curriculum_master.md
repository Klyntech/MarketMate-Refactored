## Module Template
Every module follows this exact structure:
1. Module Title
2. Learning Objectives (2–3 bullet points)
3. Reading (300–600 words)
4. Annotated Chart (single PNG, pre‑generated)
5. "Why This Matters" callout (2–3 sentences, no engine parameters)
6. Practical Exercise (identification or calculation task, no trade instructions)
7. Quiz (3–5 multiple‑choice questions)

## Quiz & Exam Rules
- Module quiz: 3–5 questions, 70% to pass, unlimited retakes, best score kept
- Final exam: 40 questions + practical chart‑marking challenge, 80% to pass
- Correct answers never revealed after failure
- No time limits
