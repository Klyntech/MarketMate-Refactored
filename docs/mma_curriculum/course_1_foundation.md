# Course 1 — Foundation (Market Structure & Price Action)

## Prerequisites: None

| Module | Title | Practical Exercise |
|--------|-------|---------------------|
| 1.1 | What Moves XAUUSD? | Label London/NY/Asia sessions on a real 24‑hour XAUUSD chart |
| 1.2 | Candlestick Anatomy | Identify bullish/bearish candles on a real chart; mark wicks and bodies |
| 1.3 | Timeframes & Their Roles | Compare the same price action on H4, M15, M5 side‑by‑side |
| 1.4 | Support & Resistance – The Foundation | Mark swing highs/lows on a 90‑day H4 chart; draw key S/R zones |
| 1.5 | Trend Structure | Identify the current H4 trend; label HH/HL or LH/LL |
| 1.6 | Sessions & Volatility Patterns | Analyse a week of XAUUSD data; highlight the most volatile hours |

## Assessment
- Quiz: 15 questions, multiple choice, 70% to pass
- Badge upon completion: Market Structure Graduate
