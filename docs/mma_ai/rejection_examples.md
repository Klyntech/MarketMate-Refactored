# MMAcademy Rejection Examples

This document contains real examples of rejected content and the specific violation each represents.
Every AI operator and human reviewer must study these before contributing.

---

## Example 1 — Terminology Violation

**Rejected text:**
"The liquidity grab at the session open took out the previous day's low before reversing."

**Why rejected:**
"Liquidity grab" is a banned term. The MMAcademy‑approved term is Liquidity Sweep.

**Correct version:**
"The liquidity sweep at the session open took out the previous day's low before reversing."

---

## Example 2 — Banned Term (Alternative)

**Rejected text:**
"Price then filled the imbalance left by the previous candle, indicating strong institutional interest."

**Why rejected:**
"Imbalance" is banned. Use Fair Value Gap (FVG).

**Correct version:**
"Price then filled the FVG left by the previous candle..."

---

## Example 3 — Motivational Language

**Rejected text:**
"Remember, every losing trade is a learning opportunity! You've got this."

**Why rejected:**
Motivational language is forbidden. The Academy maintains a professional, direct tone.

**Correct version:**
"A losing trade, when properly journaled, provides data for future improvement."

---

## Example 4 — Execution‑Oriented Phrasing

**Rejected text:**
"Now that you've spotted the sweep, place a buy limit at the order block zone and set your stop 5 pips below."

**Why rejected:**
This is an execution instruction. The Academy never tells a student to place a trade.

**Correct version:**
"A trader might consider the order block zone as a potential entry area, with a stop loss placed below the zone."

---

## Example 5 — Shortened Lesson Structure

**Rejected text (entire module):**
"Liquidity sweeps happen when price breaks a swing high or low and then reverses. This traps breakout traders. Look for sweeps before entering."

**Why rejected:**
Missing all required template fields: Learning Objectives, Annotated Chart, "Why This Matters" callout, Practical Exercise, Quiz.

---

## Example 6 — Annotated Chart Deviation

**Rejected chart description:**
"A solid red line marks the sweep level, with a green arrow for entry direction."

**Why rejected:**
Chart annotation rules are fixed: sweep levels use dashed gold lines, not solid red. Entry arrows are never shown in Academy charts.

---

## Example 7 — Hype Language

**Rejected text:**
"This setup alone could have made you 2% on your account. Incredible, right?"

**Why rejected:**
Hype language, suggested profitability, and an exclamation mark. All prohibited.

---

## Example 8 — Gender‑Specific Pronoun

**Rejected text:**
"The trader must protect his capital above all else."

**Why rejected:**
Must use gender‑neutral pronouns.

**Correct version:**
"The trader must protect their capital above all else."

---

## Example 9 — Real‑Time Price Mention

**Rejected text:**
"Currently, XAUUSD is trading at $2,350, approaching the identified order block."

**Why rejected:**
The Academy never displays real‑time price data.

---

## Example 10 — Skipping Disclaimer

**Rejected: entire module published without the mandatory footer disclaimer.**

**Why rejected:**
Every module must end with: "This content is for educational purposes only and does not constitute financial advice. Trading involves substantial risk."
