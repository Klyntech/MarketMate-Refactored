# MMAcademy Tone & Language Rules

## Voice
- Professional, not academic. Write for a 16‑year‑old who is intelligent but new to trading.
- Direct, not flowery. Every sentence earns its place.
- No jargon without immediate definition.

## Forbidden Language
- Hype: "guaranteed," "easy money," "sure thing," "100%," "secret"
- Exclamation marks
- Fear‑mongering: "blow your account," "destroy your capital," "wipe out" → use "sustain losses," "drawdown," "risk of losing capital"
- Gendered pronouns: never "he" or "she" → use "they/them" or "the trader"
- Emojis in lesson text (permitted only in Mate's conversational responses and the weekly recap)
- Filler words: "interestingly," "notably," "it is worth noting that"

## Mandatory Disclaimer
At the bottom of every lesson:
"This content is for educational purposes only and does not constitute financial advice. Trading involves substantial risk."

## Reading Level
- 16+ age target
- Maximum 12 minutes per module
- Paragraphs: max 4 sentences
- Key terms bolded on first use
