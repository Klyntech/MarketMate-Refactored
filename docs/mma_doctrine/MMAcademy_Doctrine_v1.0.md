# MMAcademy Doctrine v1.0

## 1. Terminology Lock
Every SMC term has exactly one accepted definition within MMAcademy. No variation is permitted.

| Term | Accepted MMAcademy Definition |
|------|-------------------------------|
| Liquidity | Resting orders clustered at a price level. High liquidity facilitates smooth price movement; low liquidity causes sharp, erratic moves. |
| Liquidity Sweep | A brief push beyond a prior swing high or low that triggers resting stops, followed by a reversal. The candle's wick breaks the level; the body may or may not close back inside. |
| Inducement | The deliberate act of pushing price through a level to trigger retail stops, generating the liquidity institutions need to enter or exit large positions. |
| Order Block (OB) | The last opposing candle before a strong displacement move. A bullish OB is the last bearish candle before a bullish displacement; a bearish OB is the last bullish candle before a bearish displacement. Zone: [low, high] of that candle. |
| Fair Value Gap (FVG) | A three‑candle pattern where candle 1 and candle 3 do not overlap, leaving a price gap. Bullish FVG: candle 1.high < candle 3.low. Bearish FVG: candle 1.low > candle 3.high. Represents an imbalance of orders. |
| Break of Structure (BOS) | Price breaking beyond a prior swing point in the direction of the prevailing trend, confirming trend continuation. |
| Change of Character (CHoCH) | Price breaking beyond a prior swing point against the prevailing trend, signalling a potential reversal. |
| Swing High | A price level where the high of a candle is higher than the highs of the immediately surrounding candles (typically N candles each side). |
| Swing Low | A price level where the low of a candle is lower than the lows of the immediately surrounding candles. |
| Higher Time Frame (HTF) | Daily and 4‑hour charts. Used for bias determination and structural analysis. |
| Lower Time Frame (LTF) | 15‑minute and 5‑minute charts. Used for entry confirmation. |
| Risk‑Reward (RR) | The ratio of potential profit to potential loss on a trade. MMAcademy uses a minimum of 1:1.8. |
| Position Size | The number of lots (or units) traded, calculated as (Account Risk Amount) ÷ (Stop‑Loss Distance in Pips × Pip Value). |
| Drawdown | The peak‑to‑trough decline in account equity, measured as a percentage. |
| Confluence | Multiple independent SMC factors pointing to the same trade direction, increasing the probability of a successful setup. |

### Banned Terminology
The following terms are never to be used in MMAcademy content:
- Smart Money Trap
- Judas Swing
- Liquidity Void
- Breaker Block
- Mitigation Block
- Rejection Block
- Order Flow
- Imbalance (use Fair Value Gap)

If a student asks about any banned term, Mate responds: "That term is not part of the MarketMate SMC framework. In our Academy, we use [correct term] to describe that concept."

## 2. Naming Conventions
- Symbol: Always "XAUUSD" (not "Gold," "XAU/USD," or "spot gold" except in introductory context).
- Sessions: "London session," "New York session," "Asian session." Never "London killzone," "NY open," or "Asian range."
- Gates: Referred to as "Gate 1," "Gate 2," etc. Never "G1," "Stage 1," or "Filter 1."
- MarketMate brand: "MarketMate" (one word, capital M). "Mate" refers to the assistant. "MMAcademy" is the education division.
- Timeframes: Written as "H4," "M15," "M5," "D1." Never "4‑hour," "15‑minute," or "daily" in technical descriptions.

## 3. Chart Annotation Standards
All annotated charts used in the Academy must follow these rules:
- Background: Dark (#0A0B0C) for dark mode; off‑white (#FDFCFB) for light mode.
- Candles: Emerald green (#10B981) for bullish; red (#EF4444) for bearish.
- Markups:
  - Sweep level: Dashed gold horizontal line (#D4AF37, 1.5px, 40% opacity). Label: "Liquidity Sweep" in gold, 10px font, placed at the right end of the line.
  - OB zone: Gold rectangle (no fill, 1.5px border, 20% opacity background). Label: "Order Block" in gold.
  - FVG zone: Gold rectangle with diagonal hash fill (10% opacity). Label: "FVG" in gold.
  - BOS/CHoCH: Gold arrow pointing in the direction of the break. Label: "BOS" or "CHoCH" in gold.
- No TP/SL lines on educational charts. Those are proprietary and only appear in live signal charts sent to subscribers.
- No price labels on the annotation lines themselves (only the concept label).
- Chart watermarks: None. The MarketMate logo appears in the page header, not on the chart image.

Charts are pre‑generated by chart_renderer.py and stored in website/static/assets/learn/charts/. Every module references the exact filename. No AI‑generated charts are permitted.

## 4. Tone & Language Rules
- Professional, not academic. Write for a 16‑year‑old who is intelligent but new to trading. No jargon without immediate definition.
- Direct, not flowery. Every sentence earns its place. Avoid "interestingly," "notably," "it is worth noting that."
- No hype. Never use "guaranteed," "easy money," "sure thing," "100%," "secret," or exclamation marks.
- No fear‑mongering. Avoid "blow your account," "destroy your capital," "wipe out." Use "sustain losses," "drawdown," "risk of losing capital."
- No gendered language. Use "they/them" or "the trader." Never "he" or "she."
- No emojis in lesson text. Emojis are permitted only in Mate's conversational responses and the weekly recap.
- Disclaimers are mandatory at the bottom of every lesson: "This content is for educational purposes only and does not constitute financial advice. Trading involves substantial risk."

## 5. Educational Boundaries (What MMAcademy Never Does)
- Never tells a student to enter, manage, or exit a live trade.
- Never shows real‑time price data.
- Never reveals MarketMate's gate parameters, engine internals, or proprietary algorithms.
- Never compares MarketMate to another signal service.
- Never guarantees profitability or suggests that completing the Academy ensures trading success.
- Never uses images of luxury items, money, or lifestyle content.
- Never publishes content that has not been reviewed by at least one human after the AI pipeline.

## 6. AI Content Pipeline Rules
- Drafting model: ChatGPT (or equivalent). Receives the module brief and produces a full first draft.
- Filtering models: Three independent models (Claude, Gemini, Groq). Each receives the ChatGPT draft and flags: factual errors, terminology violations, tone violations, structure violations. All three must approve before the draft proceeds.
- Proofreader: A final human review (you or a designated editor). You stamp the module as "Approved for Publication."
- Chart generation: Never part of the AI pipeline. Charts are pre‑generated from your real candles database using chart_renderer.py.
- Version control: Every approved module is stored with a version number and date. If a module is updated, the previous version is archived. Students who completed the earlier version are not required to re‑complete.

## 7. Quiz & Exam Standards
- All questions must be answerable from the module text alone. No "trick" questions.
- Distractors must be plausible but clearly wrong to an attentive student.
- Correct answers are never revealed to the student after a failed attempt.
- Module quiz: 3‑5 questions, 70% to pass.
- Final exam: 40 questions across all four courses + a practical chart‑marking challenge, 80% to pass.
- No time limits.

## 8. Update & Governance
- This Doctrine can only be amended by Clinton.
- Any proposed change must be reviewed against existing module content for consistency. If a term is changed, all published modules must be updated to reflect the new term.
- Version history is maintained: MMAcademy_Doctrine_v1.0.md in the project repository.

## 9. Future Expansion Policy
The Academy will NOT expand sideways with additional courses in new domains. The following are permanently forbidden as course topics:
- Crypto trading
- ICT concepts or "sniper" entries
- Funded challenge bootcamps
- Psychology masterclasses marketed as "mindset"
- Any course tagged "alpha," "secrets," or "advanced patterns"

The ONLY permitted future expansions are depth layers within the existing framework:
1. MarketMate Replay Lab — practical replay environment for chart identification drills.
2. Advanced Case Library — growing repository of TP2 wins, SL losses, and no‑trade scenarios.
3. Risk & Psychology Expansion — Course 6, only after Academy matures. Covers expectancy, drawdown mathematics, emotional process control, journaling analysis, variance, consistency systems. Must follow doctrinal rules.
4. Institutional Analytics Layer — backtest interpretation, performance analytics, confluence scoring, probabilistic thinking.

Scale depth before breadth. The existing curriculum is complete.
The real bottlenecks are content quality, chart quality, review consistency, UI/UX, progression tracking, certification credibility, and operational stability — not more course topics.
