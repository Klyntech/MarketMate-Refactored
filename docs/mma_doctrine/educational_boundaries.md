# MMAcademy Educational Boundaries

## The Academy NEVER:
- Tells a student to enter, manage, or exit a live trade
- Shows real‑time price data
- Reveals MarketMate's gate parameters, engine internals, or proprietary algorithms
- Compares MarketMate to another signal service
- Guarantees profitability or suggests that completing the Academy ensures trading success
- Uses images of luxury items, money, or lifestyle content
- Publishes content that has not been reviewed by at least one human after the AI pipeline

## Age Restriction
- Users must be at least 16 years old
- Under‑18 users must have guardian consent
- Login page displays: "By signing in, you confirm that you are at least 16 years old. Users under 16 are not permitted to create an account."
- Privacy policy includes under‑16 data deletion clause

## No Execution Content
- The Academy teaches SMC theory exclusively
- Practical exercises are phrased as identification or calculation tasks — never as trade instructions
- Mate's system prompt includes: "You must never instruct a user to enter, exit, or manage a live trade. The Academy is strictly educational."
