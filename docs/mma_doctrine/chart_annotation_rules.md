# MMAcademy Chart Annotation Rules

## General
- Background: Dark (#0A0B0C) for dark mode; off‑white (#FDFCFB) for light mode.
- Candles: Emerald green (#10B981) for bullish; red (#EF4444) for bearish.
- Charts are pre‑generated by chart_renderer.py and stored in website/static/assets/learn/charts/.
- Every module references the exact filename. AI must never generate chart images.
- No watermarks on educational charts. The MarketMate logo appears only in the page header.

## Concept Markups

### Liquidity Sweep
- Dashed gold horizontal line (#D4AF37, 1.5px, 40% opacity)
- Label: "Liquidity Sweep" in gold, 10px font, placed at the right end of the line

### Order Block (OB)
- Gold rectangle, no fill, 1.5px border, 20% opacity background
- Label: "Order Block" in gold

### Fair Value Gap (FVG)
- Gold rectangle with diagonal hash fill, 10% opacity
- Label: "FVG" in gold

### BOS / CHoCH
- Gold arrow pointing in the direction of the break
- Label: "BOS" or "CHoCH" in gold

## Prohibited on Educational Charts
- TP/SL lines (proprietary — only in subscriber signal charts)
- Price labels on annotation lines (concept labels only, no numbers)
- Watermarks or logos on the chart image itself
