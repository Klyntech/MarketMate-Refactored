# MMAcademy Terminology Lock

## Approved Terms (binding)

| Term | Definition |
|------|-----------|
| Liquidity | Resting orders clustered at a price level. High liquidity facilitates smooth price movement; low liquidity causes sharp, erratic moves. |
| Liquidity Sweep | A brief push beyond a prior swing high or low that triggers resting stops, followed by a reversal. The candle's wick breaks the level; the body may or may not close back inside. |
| Inducement | The deliberate act of pushing price through a level to trigger retail stops, generating the liquidity institutions need to enter or exit large positions. |
| Order Block (OB) | The last opposing candle before a strong displacement move. Bullish OB: last bearish candle before a bullish displacement. Bearish OB: last bullish candle before a bearish displacement. Zone: [low, high] of that candle. |
| Fair Value Gap (FVG) | Three‑candle pattern where candle 1 and candle 3 do not overlap, leaving a price gap. Bullish FVG: candle 1.high < candle 3.low. Bearish FVG: candle 1.low > candle 3.high. |
| Break of Structure (BOS) | Price breaking beyond a prior swing point in the direction of the prevailing trend, confirming continuation. |
| Change of Character (CHoCH) | Price breaking beyond a prior swing point against the prevailing trend, signalling a potential reversal. |
| Swing High | The high of a candle that is higher than the highs of the immediately surrounding candles. |
| Swing Low | The low of a candle that is lower than the lows of the immediately surrounding candles. |
| Higher Time Frame (HTF) | Daily and 4‑hour charts. |
| Lower Time Frame (LTF) | 15‑minute and 5‑minute charts. |
| Risk‑Reward (RR) | Ratio of potential profit to potential loss. MMAcademy minimum: 1:1.8. |
| Position Size | (Account Risk Amount) ÷ (Stop‑Loss Distance in Pips × Pip Value). |
| Drawdown | Peak‑to‑trough decline in account equity, measured as a percentage. |
| Confluence | Multiple independent SMC factors pointing to the same trade direction. |
